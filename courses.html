Everything you need to know before enrolling

What documents are required for enrollment?
No documents are required for a regular enrollment. You just need to register on the platform and complete the payment. For scholarship eligibility, an Aadhaar card and 10th mark sheet may be needed.
When will I get access to the batch after enrollment?
Access to your batch will be provided within 24 hours of successful payment. You'll receive a confirmation email with login details.
Are the classes live or recorded?
We offer both live and recorded classes. Live sessions are interactive with real-time doubt solving. All live lectures are recorded and available on the platform within 4 hours.
What is the refund policy?
Refund requests must be raised within 7 days of enrollment. Registration fees and study material costs are non-refundable. Please contact our support center for detailed refund procedures.
Can I access courses on mobile?
Yes, all courses are fully accessible on mobile, tablet, and desktop. You can learn anytime, anywhere with our responsive platform.
