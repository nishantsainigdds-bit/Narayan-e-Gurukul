About

About e-Gurukul Rank Predictor

The e-Gurukul Rank Predictor is a free tool designed to help students estimate their rank in JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and NEET UG before the official results are declared. By entering your expected or actual score, the tool uses historical cutoff data and rank distribution patterns to give you a reliable rank estimate.

The rank predictor uses previous year rank vs score data from official NTA and IIT JEE records to calculate your estimated rank. It also factors in category-wise cutoffs to give you a more accurate prediction based on your reservation category.

This tool is especially useful for students who want to shortlist colleges, plan their counselling strategy, and understand where they stand before the official results. Remember, this is an estimate — actual ranks may vary based on the difficulty level of the exam and the number of candidates appearing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the e-Gurukul Rank Predictor?
The rank predictor uses previous year data and statistical models to estimate your rank. It is generally accurate within a ±10-15% range. Actual ranks depend on the current year's difficulty level and total candidates.
What is the percentile to qualify for JEE Advanced?
For General category, you need approximately 88-90 percentile in JEE Main to qualify for JEE Advanced. For OBC-NCL it's around 74%, SC around 54%, and ST around 44%. These cutoffs vary each year.
What rank can I expect with 270 marks in JEE Advanced?
With 270 marks out of 360 in JEE Advanced, you can expect a rank in the range of 50–300 (General category). This would likely qualify you for top IITs like IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, or IIT Madras for popular branches.
What score is needed for AIIMS Delhi in NEET?
To get AIIMS Delhi (MBBS), you typically need a score of 700+ out of 720 in NEET UG, which corresponds to a rank within the top 50. The cutoff varies slightly each year.
Can I use this tool after the exam?
Yes, you can use this tool both before and after the exam. After the exam, enter your calculated score based on the official answer key to get a more accurate rank prediction.
